## Upload policy

Allow nonempty PDF/PNG/JPEG up to 5 MiB; extension, declared MIME and detected content must agree. Plain PDF inspection rejects encryption, active actions/attachments/forms and over 40 pages, with bounded object traversal. Images are verified/decoded, single-frame, and limited to 20 million pixels. A subprocess limits CPU to five seconds and address space to 512 MiB; the parent enforces an eight-second wall timeout.

File names are randomized, metadata/checksum is recorded, and downloads require dedicated permission plus shipment/document view permissions. Files have no public URL and no public rendering route. This is structural validation, not malware scanning, OCR, AI extraction or content sanitization certification. Those future document operations must keep the same private storage and permission boundaries.
